# ADR-0012: "Decided against" shown neutrally; no streaks, trophies or celebration

**Status**: accepted
**Type**: product
**Date**: 2026-10-04

## Context

Showing "money not spent" motivates, but research on moral licensing shows that resisting a purchase can make the next indulgence feel deserved. Gamified totals also invite adding fake wishes to grow the number.

## Decision

- Rejected items are kept in a "Decided against" history with a plain total ("You decided against 3,420 € of purchases").
- No streaks, badges, confetti or "you saved!" wording anywhere.
- The total is not shown next to Ready items.

## Consequences

- Less "dopamine", more honesty; consistent with "better spending, not maximum frugality".
