# ADR-0013: While cooling off — reasons first, image de-emphasized, no "open in shop" button

**Status**: accepted
**Type**: product
**Date**: 2026-10-04

## Context

Product images and imagining using the product increase desire. Browsing during a delay defeats the delay. Earmarking research suggests a goal image can help saving.

## Decision

- While an item is cooling off, item detail leads with the user's reasons and the countdown; the image is small or collapsed.
- No prominent "open in shop" button while cooling off; the link stays reachable via a menu.
- Images may be shown larger in the savings view once the item is Saving or Ready.

## Consequences

- Revisit with real usage data; the image trade-off is an informed guess.
