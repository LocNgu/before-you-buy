# Before You Buy

*(working name)*

An Android app that helps you **pause, reflect, prioritize and deliberately save** before you buy things.

> Don't prevent people from buying things. Help them make sure the purchase is actually worth it.

Instead of *"Can I afford this?"* it asks **"Is this the best use of my money compared with everything else I want?"**

- **Cooling-off periods** scaled by price (e.g. 7 days under 100 €, 90 days over 1,000 €)
- **Reflection** — why you want it, how often you'll use it, reasons against
- **Priorities** — a few active slots; a new want has to win a head-to-head to get one
- **Savings per item** from a monthly discretionary allowance — an item is *Ready* when it's cooled off and funded
- **Decision screen** with opportunity cost: Buy, Wait or Reject
- **History** of what you bought and what you decided against
- **Private by design** — local only, no account, no tracking

Status: **planning** — see [`docs/product-brief.md`](docs/product-brief.md), [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/README.md), [`docs/research.md`](docs/research.md) and [`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md). Work is tracked in GitHub issues.
